Case Compass – Quick Start

Document facts. Preserve evidence. Protect safety. Organize next steps.

In an emergency, call 911. Pueblo domestic violence hotline (Mariposa, 24 hours): 719-545-8195 · National DV Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 (text START to 88788) · Crisis: call or text 988.

1. Install

  1. iPhone/iPad: open the Case Compass address in Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen. Always open it from the icon.
  2. Computer: open the address in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox (or run python3 -m http.server 8080 in the folder and visit localhost:8080).

2. Create your vault

  1. Choose a passphrase of 12+ characters — four or more random words work well.
  2. Write it down and keep it safe. It cannot be recovered or reset.
  3. Everything stays encrypted on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

3. Your first week

  1. Make an encrypted backup (Reports › Backup) and store it off this device.
  2. Add each child (Parenting › Children).
  3. Enter court dates and deadlines from official papers, with their source.
  4. Record the most recent important events, newest first.
  5. Add existing screenshots, photos, and documents as evidence.
  6. Open Protection & Security and pick the protocol that matters most.
  7. Write attorney questions in the Attorney Workspace.

Writing good records

  • Write soon, with dates, times, and places.
  • Say what you saw yourself vs. what you were told.
  • Quote exact words only when you are sure.
  • Describe actions, not labels or conclusions.
  • Never alter originals; add edited copies separately.
  • Never coach or question children about the case.

Keep it private

  • The vault locks automatically when idle; tap the padlock to lock now.
  • Quick Exit (top right, or Esc ×3) locks and opens a neutral site. It cannot erase browser history or downloads.
  • Reports and CSV files are not encrypted. Delete copies you no longer need.
  • Account changes can notify other people. Preserve evidence first and plan timing with an advocate if safety is a concern.
  • If the device may be monitored, use a trusted device when you can.

Back up often

  • Reports › Backup › Create encrypted backup. The file is checked before you download it.
  • On iPhone choose Share › Save to Files.
  • New phone? Copy the backup there and choose Restore from an encrypted backup.

Deadlines and reminders

  • Enter court dates only from official notices or orders, and record the source. The app never calculates deadlines.
  • The app can't alert you while closed: use Add to device calendar for phone reminders.

Reports for an attorney

  • Home › Attorney Workspace › Create packet.
  • Private notes and detailed child information are left out unless you include them.
  • Reports are your summaries, not court filings or evidence by themselves.
